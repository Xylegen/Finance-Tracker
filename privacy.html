Home Ledger: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Home Ledger is a personal finance tracker built and used by one person. It has no other users and is not distributed.

Data the app accesses

With its owner's consent, Home Ledger uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope (gmail.readonly) to read transaction alert emails sent by banks and card issuers to the owner's own Gmail account.

How the data is used

The contents of those emails are used only to build the owner's personal record of transactions. The app does not send, modify or delete email.

Storage and sharing

Data read from Gmail is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to third parties. Google user data is not used to develop or train generalised AI or machine learning models.

Home Ledger's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the app's local database, and can revoke the app's access from their Google Account's security settings.

Contact

Home Ledger has a single user, who is also its developer. Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
